# Executive Client Profile

## Client

United Amara Bank (UAB)

## Industry

Commercial Banking

## Engagement

Executive Financial Performance Review (FY2020–FY2024)

## Role

Business Analyst / Business Intelligence Consultant

## Business Need

Senior management requires a consolidated view of the bank's financial performance to monitor growth, profitability, financial position and risk across multiple financial years.

## Project Objective

Design an interactive Power BI dashboard that transforms published financial statements into actionable management insights.

## Data Source

- UAB Annual Reports (FY2020–FY2024)
- UAB Audited Financial Statements (FY2020–FY2024)

## Expected Deliverables

- Executive Dashboard
- Financial Performance Analysis
- Risk Analysis
- Strategic Recommendations
